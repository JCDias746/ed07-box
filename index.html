Built, Not Given

He funds the dream himself.

Karting costs more than most families can carry, and nobody wrote this one a cheque. A ten-year-old could have quit. Instead, Enrique got to work. He prints and sells his own 3D toys, dragons and keychains, and the Built, Not Given hoodies and tees take it further. What started on a school desk is a real business, and every single sale buys another lap toward Formula 1.

That is what Built, Not Given means. Nobody handed him the dream. He is building it, one lap and one sale at a time.
